  1. Upload at photolab.londondrugs.com and order the size you picked above, quantity one per file. 4 × 6 is under Prints; 8 × 12 and 12 × 18 are under Enlargements. Pearl finish has less glare behind glass; glossy is fine too. Pick up in store.
  2. Cut along the thin grey lines with a paper cutter or scissors. Each tile is 2.4 × 3.6 in, about ³⁄₁₆ in bigger than the opening on every side. Cut on the line or a hair outside it, never inside. Only filled windows are printed, packed in order.
  3. Lay the mat face down. Place each tile face down over its opening and tape the top edge to the back of the mat. Each tile has its number printed in the top-left corner, in the strip the mat hides.
